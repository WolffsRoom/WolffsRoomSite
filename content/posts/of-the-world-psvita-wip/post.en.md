Hey everyone! Wolff here!

**Of the World** is coming to **PlayStation Vita**!

Even though it's a **Game Boy Color** game that can be easily emulated, I wanted to take a more native approach: recompiling it for the Vita to make **widescreen adaptation** easier and simplify distribution. The goal is to make it as straightforward as installing **a single VPK**.

More updates are coming soon, hopefully along with a **playable beta build**!

If everything goes well, I plan to bring **Temmie's other games** to PS Vita through recompilation too.

**Take care, everyone!**
