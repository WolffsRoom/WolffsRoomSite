Unofficial native port of Undertale Yellow. It runs the full game on a custom Butterscotch build with hardware-accelerated rendering through VitaGL. Undertale Yellow is a free fangame, a prequel to UNDERTALE, made by Team Undertale Yellow.

## Highlights
- Modular translation and mod support in ux0:data/undertale-yellow/mods/Lang/
- Languages show up under Game Settings > Language (Select in game)
- Port-specific settings in the Game Settings menu

## Requirements
- kubridge (kubridge.skprx)
- FdFix (fd_fix.skprx), unless you use rePatch
- libshacccg.suprx installed on the Vita
- PSVshell (optional) to overclock the CPU in heavy battles
