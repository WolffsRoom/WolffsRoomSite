Unofficial native port of Void Stranger. It does not emulate Windows: it reads the official GameMaker data (Steam 1.1.3) and runs it on a custom Butterscotch build, with VitaGL and OpenAL. The game is fully playable; FPS may drop in battles with many effects.

## Highlights
- Native controls and touch support
- Six languages, including Brazilian Portuguese
- Vita-specific graphics options and palette/tone controls
- Texture cache built on the console itself
- Intro video with native playback
- 30 native trophies, with an in-game trophy browser

## Requirements
- Legitimate copy of Void Stranger 1.1.3 on Steam
- kubridge (kubridge.skprx)
- FdFix (fd_fix.skprx), unless you use rePatch
- libshacccg.suprx installed on the Vita
- NoTrpDrm (optional, for the native trophies)
