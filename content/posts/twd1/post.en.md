Telltale's narrative adventure follows Lee Everett as he protects Clementine during a zombie apocalypse. Player choices shape their relationships and the story.

## About this translation

GameLab Traduções reviewed the Brazilian Portuguese localization, correcting text and adapting 80 textures and 7 fonts for accented characters.

## Installation

- You must own game version **PCSE00316** and have the **rePatch** plugin installed and enabled.
- Download the RAR from one of the providers, and extract it on a computer using password **wolffsroom**.
- Open the extracted **CONSOLE** directory and copy the **rePatch** folder to **ux0:** over USB or FTP; translated files must end up inside **ux0:rePatch/PCSE00316/**.
- Back up any existing mod for this TitleID, restart your Vita and launch the game.

Automated installation remains disabled in the Vita Store until verified RAR handling and safe mod backups are supported.
