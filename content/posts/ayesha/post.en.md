Brazilian Portuguese translation of Atelier Ayesha on PS Vita. Since it is a large JRPG, the project may take years, or may never be finished.

## About the project
- A Python script extracts the .ebm files and converts them to .txt
- Line-by-line translation tool (incomplete), based on Ferripro321's Atelier Translator
