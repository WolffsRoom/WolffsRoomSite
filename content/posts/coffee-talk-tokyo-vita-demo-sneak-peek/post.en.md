Hey everyone! Wolff here!

I have another project to share with you: **Coffee Talk Tokyo is being adapted for PlayStation Vita!**

I'm working through the final adjustments and fixes needed to release a **free demo**. It's already reached gameplay on real hardware, but there are still a few things to review before publishing it. At the same time, I'm also working on the **full game**, which will come later since it needs considerably more development, testing, and validation.

This is **my first Unity project**! I'm backporting the game from a modern version of the engine, **Unity 6 (the 2024 generation)**, to **Unity 2017**, which supports native PS Vita development. That means adapting scenes, interfaces, scripts, effects, and other systems to a much older runtime and to the handheld's limitations.

My ambition goes beyond this one title: **I'd love to bring the entire Coffee Talk catalog to PS Vita**. Starting with the newest game is a way to demonstrate that the idea is feasible, even though there's still plenty of work to do.

The demo will be the first step. Work on the full version will continue until it's ready for a later release.

**The free demo is coming soon to Wolff's Store!**

Take care, everyone, and see you soon!
