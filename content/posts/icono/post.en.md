Brazilian Portuguese translation of Iconoclasts on PS Vita: the PC translation files by Tribo Gamer and Central de Traduções were ported to the console. No graphics were redrawn, so some words remain in English.

## Requirements
- Game installed on the Vita (USA, PCSE01177)
- rePatch plugin
- VitaShell, to create folders and extract the ZIP

## Installation
- Download the archive and extract it with VitaShell
- Create the rePatch folder in ux0:
- Copy the PCSE01177 folder to ux0:rePatch/
- Restart the Vita

## Known issues
- Some text is hard to read
- Awkward line breaks
- Portuguese mistakes and confusing sentences
