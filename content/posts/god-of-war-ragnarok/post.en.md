God of War Ragnarök follows **Kratos and Atreus** on the final stretch of their Norse adventure. This personal review was first published on **January 26, 2025** after **48 hours on PlayStation 4**. It uses the author's *Six Cubed* method: six categories, each worth up to 36 points.

## Graphics — Perfect (36/36)
- Beautiful PS4 environments, full of detail in water, forests, boats and the Norse realms.
- Strong art direction rewards exploration and offers plenty of memorable scenes.

## Story — Great (30/36)
- Kratos and Atreus grow through emotional developments, revelations and new encounters.
- A few story arcs slow the pacing and the ending feels rushed.

## Gameplay — Perfect (36/36)
- Satisfying combat expands on God of War (2018), with smooth weapon switching and more combat options.
- Equipment progression and upgrades remain overly complicated.

## Soundtrack — Perfect (36/36)
- **Bear McCreary's** score uses epic choirs and Norse-inspired instrumentation.
- Music heightens both combat intensity and emotional scenes.

## Level design — Great (30/36)
- Distinct realms, secrets and puzzles make exploration rewarding.
- Backtracking and some long optional quests, particularly in Vanaheim, diminish the pacing.
- The world could benefit from more characters to interact with.

## Length — Great (30/36)
- The optional activities enrich the campaign without excessive padding.
- My playthrough took **48 hours** without focusing on platinum trophies.

## Verdict — 198/216
- **Recommended!** An excellent follow-up with powerful combat and a richly detailed world despite minor pacing and progression problems.
- Original score: **198/216**, approximately **9.2/10**.
