Brazilian Portuguese translation of Cave Story, adapted for PS Vita on NXEngine. Includes its own VPK, edited artwork and a LiveArea manual in progress.

## Features
- Widescreen support
- Improved dialogue boxes and animations
- Better menu and save management
- Mod support
- Runs at a locked 55 FPS, like the original

## Installation
- Download and extract Cave Story.zip
- Send CaveStory.vpk to the Vita over FTP or USB
- Install it with VitaShell
- If you already have an English save, back up ux0:user/00 first
