Unofficial PS Vita port of the Pizza Tower demo released at the 2019 Sonic Amateur Games Expo (SAGE). The demo is free and made in GameMaker; the port uses its data, which each player downloads from itch.io.

## About the demo
- Released during SAGE 2019, with new levels, the Snick Challenge mode and plenty of unused content
- Snick the Porcupine is playable only in this demo
- Pizza coins drop from blocks and buy the shotgun at the Pizzamart

## Requirements
- Hacked PS Vita
- The PizzaTowerSAGE2019_v14.zip file, downloaded from the demo's official itch.io page
