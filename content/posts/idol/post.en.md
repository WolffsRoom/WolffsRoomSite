Port of The Case of the Golden Idol to PS Vita, adapted from the Godot-based PC version. The repository gathers the scripts, patchers and tools made to run the game natively on the console. The translation is still in progress (accent support in the fonts).

## Vita improvements
- Virtual cursor on the analog stick, with adjustable speed
- Controls remapped to the Vita buttons
- Help screen showing the real console controls
- Progressive zoom on the scene and the deduction board (up to 3x)
- Screens reworked for touch

## Planned
- v0.8.0 — trophies and performance on scene transitions
- v0.9.0 — soundtrack DLC support
- v1.0.0 — language switch in the patcher

## Known issues
- Screens and levels take a little longer to load (about 5 seconds)

## Installation
- Download GoldenIdol-Patch.zip and the VPK from the Releases tab
- Extract the patch into a folder on your PC and follow the repository guide
