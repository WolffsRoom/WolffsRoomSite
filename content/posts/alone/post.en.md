Brazilian Portuguese translation of Alone With You, a science-fiction adventure by BancyCo. The game uses GameMaker, and its text is accessed with Undertale Mod Tool. The first pass was machine-translated and will be reviewed line by line against the English original.

## Plans
- Adjust the font to support accented characters
- Build a Python tool for the review
- Translate sprites, images and manuals

## Known issues
- Some sounds don't play
- Some line breaks are wrong
- The font in some titles doesn't show accents
