**TouchOnVita** is an unofficial touchscreen plugin for **Darkest Dungeon on PS Vita**, adding direct front-touch interaction and a themed settings panel.

## Working features

- Single-tap selection and activation of recognized interface widgets.
- Persistent touch focus, with handoff back to the physical controller.
- TouchOnVita panel with ON/OFF indicator, **Start + L** shortcut and transitions.

## Version and limitations

**v0.59** is installed and has been smoke-tested on real hardware with **PCSE00919 update 1.17**. Single-tap interaction received positive feedback in v0.58; v0.59 audio and FPS optimizations still require full on-device verification.

Map dragging, drag-and-drop and some advanced flows are not yet fully supported. Game archives and saves are not modified.

## Installation

In **Wolff's Store v1.0.4 or newer**, choose **Download only** (saves the ZIP under ux0:Downloads/Plugins/) or **Download and install** (backs up taiHEN and installs the plugin and assets). Reboot the PS Vita after first-time installation. Older Store versions support download only; follow the GitHub manual instructions.

No commercial game data or copyrighted audio is included.
