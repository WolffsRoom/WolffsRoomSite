Brazilian Portuguese translation of Sonic Mania Plus on PS Vita, with extra modifications. The translation is added to the game files as a mod. It is not finished or available for download yet.

## Work in progress

- Translating sprites that are still in English
- Optional removal of the 3D stages, recommended to avoid lag
- Control improvements
