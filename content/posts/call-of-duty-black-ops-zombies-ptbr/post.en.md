**Call of Duty: Black Ops Zombies** is a mobile adaptation of the Call of Duty Zombies mode. It includes the original **Kino der Toten** map from Call of Duty: Black Ops and **50 levels of Dead-Ops Arcade**.

It is available on **PlayStation Vita** with a complete Brazilian Portuguese translation by **GameLab Traduções**.

## About the translation

The PS Vita port was developed by **devnoname120**, with Brazilian Portuguese localization by **GameLab Traduções** and work by **MeninoSung**.
